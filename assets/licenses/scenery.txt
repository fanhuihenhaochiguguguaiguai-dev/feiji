V18.3 environment sources

Relief for the coastal and alpine areas and hangar ridge geometry adapted from the U.S. Geological Survey 3D Elevation Program, National Map seamless elevation service. Public-domain source data. These scenes are fictional adaptations with remapped elevation, an authored alpine flight corridor and approximated shoreline depth.
https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program/about-3dep-products-services
https://elevation.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevation/ImageServer
Source survey request bbox -136.62,58.43,-135.87,58.82 (EPSG:4326). The service adjusted the delivered raster to -136.62,58.25,-135.87,59.00 to preserve its square pixels. Fetched on 2026-11-25, 1281 x 1281 float GeoTIFF with bilinear sampling.
The alpine sample uses a separate 1100 x 1100 float GeoTIFF fetched from the same service
on 2026-11-25 at -136.515,58.82525,-136.2525,58.9535 (EPSG:4326), over the same terrain footprint.
The half-snow accumulation field is derived from that existing relief and the licensed rock scan.
It uses altitude, exposure, local slope, catchment curvature, and estimated horizon shelter.

Sky photography by Poly Haven, CC0 1.0. Original HDR files resampled to 1024 x 512 and packed with an exposure shoulder into sRGB PNG. Sky shaders and probes restore the stored linear exposure range. The sky and sun use the same panorama alignment.
https://polyhaven.com/a/qwantani_sunset_puresky
https://polyhaven.com/a/qwantani_dusk_2_puresky
https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky
https://polyhaven.com/license

Rock 04 and Gravelly Sand textures by Poly Haven, CC0 1.0. The existing 1K diffuse and GL normal are packed with the original 1K displacement and roughness maps. See PolyHaven-CC0.txt for asset links.
The alpine snow texture uses the CC0 gravel color and displacement as its grain source.

Volumetric smoke and sustained flame: Small Campfire by JangaFX, generated in
EmberGen and released under CC0 1.0. Adapted to a pair of RGB light-response
atlases with opacity in alpha, and a separate rendered emission atlas.
Six sampled density and flame grids from the same VDB are packed with
integrated upper-hemisphere visibility for close-range destruction smoke.
https://jangafx.com/software/embergen/download/free-vdb-animations

Short impact fireball: adapted from the Lave Blob sequence in Photo Realistic
Explosions by fscopel, Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. Changes include frame
selection, black background removal, color grading and atlas packing.
https://opengameart.org/content/photo-realistic-explosions
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
